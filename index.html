Buy with care

Confidence comes from checking the details.

Before committing to a vehicle, take time to verify its condition, identity, documentation and total purchase costs.

  • Compare the vehicle's physical details with its registration records.
  • Arrange an independent mechanical inspection where appropriate.
  • Review available service, ownership and accident-history information.
  • Confirm import and customs documents for imported vehicles.
  • Agree on the final price, fees and payment terms in writing.
  • Confirm the official ownership transfer process before handover.
  • Arrange legally required motor insurance before road use.
